## 🔄 Preprocessing Workflow

![Preprocessing Flowchart](flowchart/_Flowchart_Manalo_Mercado.png)

### 🧭 Steps

1. 📥 **Load the dataset:** read the raw pizza order records into DataFrame `df` containing all 12 columns.
2. 🔎 **Inspect the data:** `shape`, `head()`, `info()`, `dtypes`, `describe()`.
3. 🕳️ **Handle missing values** by column role (IDs & dates, pricing & quantity, categoricals, text).
4. 👯 **Deduplicate and consolidate order lines** (exact full-row duplicates and partial key collisions).
5. 🔢 **Convert column data types** (dates & times, numerics, categoricals, IDs & text).
6. 📈 **Filter invalid records and cap extreme outliers** (invalid values, formula audit, statistical outliers).
7. 🏷️ **Encode categorical columns:** `pizza_size` (ordinal), `pizza_category` (one-hot), `pizza_name` and `pizza_name_id`, `pizza_ingredients`.
8. ⚖️ **Scale numerical columns** (target features, method, exclusions: ___).
9. 💾 **Save the cleaned dataset** with `df.to_csv('pizza_orders_cleaned.csv', index=False)`.
10. 📊 **Visualization loop:** make plot, caption, save, repeated until `i = 10`.

### 🔀 Decisions in our flowchart

| # | Decision diamond | Condition checked | ✅ YES branch | ❌ NO branch |
|---|------------------|-------------------|--------------|-------------|
| 1 | 🕳️ **Missing values?** | `df.isnull().sum() > 0` across any of the 12 columns | Handle missing values by column role, then continue | Skip to duplicate check |
| 2 | 👯 **Duplicate rows?** | `df.duplicated().sum() > 0` | Drop exact duplicates and resolve partial key collisions, then continue | Skip to data type conversion |
| 3 | 📈 **Outliers detected?** | IQR / Z-score check on `quantity`, `unit_price`, `total_price` | Filter invalid records and cap statistical outliers, then continue | Skip to categorical encoding |
| 4 | 📊 **More plots?** | `i < 10` | Return to the visualization loop | Exit loop and go to **END** 🏁 |
